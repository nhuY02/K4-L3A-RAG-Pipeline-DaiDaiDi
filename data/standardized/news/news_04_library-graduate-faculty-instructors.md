# Graduate students, faculty, and instructors

Information for graduate students, faculty, and instructors on borrowing, resources, services, and spaces at the VinUni Library.

## Borrowing

### My library card

The VinUniversity ID serves as the library card, used to borrow library materials or equipment.

### Borrowing privileges

Graduate students may borrow up to 5 items at a time for up to one month per item. Books may be renewed once for two weeks, provided no other patron has requested the item. Materials that are not overdue may be renewed by phone or in person at the circulation desk. Overdue materials must be renewed in person at the circulation desk.

### Requests and hold

- Books can be requested from the VinUni Library online catalogue. Requests take one to two business days, and the patron is notified by email when items are ready for pickup. Status is also shown in the library account.
- Books can be borrowed at the circulation counter, and two self-check machines are available.
- Items are held for two days.

### Fines and other charges

- Fines apply for late returns and for damaged or lost materials or equipment, and follow VinUni's Financial Regulations and Tariff.
- Overdue fines are 10,000 VND per business day after the due date, per item. They accrue from the due date to the date the item is reported lost, or returned if found afterwards.
- Appeals are made by email to the library and handled case by case.

### Equipment loans

The following equipment can be borrowed from the Circulation Desk for use in the library only: mouse, headphones, iPads, memory sticks (USB), and mobile device chargers. The loan period for most items is three hours. They are free to borrow, but borrowers cover the cost of lost or stolen items.

Late return fines are 10,000 VND per hour or part of an hour. The cost of replacement parts is determined by library staff.

## Resources

### Find resources

- Ask a Librarian: help with research.
- Catalogue: books, journal articles, journals, and other library materials.
- Databases: search by topic for journal articles, conference proceedings, and more.
- Research Guides: resources and tips for completing research.
- E-journals: the library's subscription e-journals on a variety of subjects.
- New Arrivals: resources to access current and older news sources.

### Remote access to resources

- Current students, faculty, staff, and certain community users may access online resources from anywhere, logging in with a username and password through the Remote Access button.
- On a public computer, users must close the browser after use to prevent misuse of library resources.

### Use online resources

- Use of electronic resources (databases, e-journals, etc.) is limited to authorized VinUni users (students, faculty, staff, and staff from Vingroup's P&Ls and other counterparts) and is governed by license agreements between VinUni and the publishers.
- Users log in with a username and password both on and off campus.
- Users must follow copyright law and license provisions. Database material must be used for personal, non-commercial purposes.
- Unauthorized use can lead to revocation of access for the whole university, disciplinary action, or referral to civil authorities.
- The Library supports Open Access and Open Education Resources (see the Open Scholarship Guide).

### Copyright

Library resources are governed by copyright law and licenses; the Intellectual Property Law of Vietnam governs the use of online and print resources. The Library advises faculty, staff, and students, including on fair use. Many Open Access resources use a Creative Commons license.

### Suggest a resource

The library welcomes suggestions for materials it does not own, through the online suggest-a-resource form.

### Academic footprint

The page links to external guides on calculating and tracking an academic footprint (citation counts, journal impact factors, article downloads, h-index), from the University of Waterloo and the University of Windsor.

### Research data management

The page links to Harvard Dataverse (an open repository for sharing, preserving, and citing research data) and DMPOnline (a cloud-based tool for preparing data management plans, provided by the Digital Curation Centre).

## Services

### Accessibility services

The library is committed to accommodating diverse accessibility needs. Users with accessibility needs should contact the Student Affairs Office.

### Printing, copying, and scanning

The library has two multifunctional printers for printing, scanning, and copying documents.

### Computers

Users may use the library desktop computers, borrow laptops or iPads from the circulation desk, or connect their own devices to the University Wi-Fi. All University computing equipment is governed by the University's acceptable use policy.

## Spaces

### Spaces

The library offers 4 main types of spaces supporting learning, teaching, research, innovation, and entrepreneurial activities: collaboration spaces, consultation spaces, sanctuary spaces, and community engagement spaces.

### Location

The library is at the center of campus, with floor plans and a campus map available online.

### Hours

- Main entrance: in semester 8 am – 9 pm (Mon–Fri) and 9 am – 5 pm (Sat–Sun); exam period/summer to be determined; closed on national holidays.
- Second floor entrance: in semester Mon–Fri 8 am – 5 pm; Sat–Sun closed (open only on working Saturdays 8:30 am – 5:30 pm); exam period/summer 8:30 am – 5:30 pm; closed on holidays.
- 24/7 learning space: available.

### Study rooms

Study rooms are for group use by graduate and undergraduate students, faculty, and staff only, booked online with a University ID.

- First come, first served.
- Each group may book up to 2 hours per session, 2 sessions per day, and 4 sessions per week, all rooms combined.
- Reservations may be made on the same day or at most 1 week in advance.
- A reservation is cancelled if the group is more than 10 minutes late, and another group may take the room.
- Study rooms are for group study only: at least 2 people must be present. If only one person remains for more than 10 minutes mid-session, another group may take the room for the remaining time.

## Get help

- Ask a librarian and research guides are available online.
- Academic librarians present workshops and library tours for students, faculty, and staff. To request one, contact the Director, Library and Learning Resources.
